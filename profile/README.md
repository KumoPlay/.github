Hi, and welcome to the cloud! 🌸

**KumoPlay** is a tiny indie studio making small, cozy games you can pick up anytime: while you wait for the bus, on a tea break, or just before bed. Play one round and put it down, or keep coming back day after day. There's no rush, and nobody to beat.

*Kumo* (雲) means "cloud" in Japanese. Like a cloud, our games are light and gentle, and they'll still be there whenever you look up again.

## How to play

1. Visit **[kumoplay.github.io](https://kumoplay.github.io)** on your phone, tablet, or computer.
2. Pick a game that fits your moment:
   - **Quick** games are one round and done, perfect for a short wait.
   - **Endless** games are ones you can keep coming back to.
3. Each game page shows how long a round usually takes. Tap **Play now** and have fun! 💜

Out now:

- 🌾 **[Kumo Farm](https://kumoplay.github.io/games/kumo-farm/)**: grow a cozy little farm on a floating cloud island.
- ☕ **[Kumo Café](https://kumoplay.github.io/games/kumo-cafe/)**: cook and serve breakfast at a cozy café in the clouds.
- 🔍 **[Peek-a-Kumo](https://kumoplay.github.io/games/peek-a-kumo/)**: find hidden treasures in a little town on the clouds.
- 🫧 **[Bubble Pop](https://kumoplay.github.io/games/bubble-pop/)**: pop bubble wrap, blow soap bubbles and squish a cloud in a cozy pocket fidget toy. Our newest!

New games float in regularly. The website always has the full list, plus what's new and what's coming soon.
