# Contributing to KumoPlay

Thank you for wanting to help! 💜 KumoPlay is a small studio, and every bug report, idea, and kind word makes our games better.

You don't need to know how to code to help. Here's how you can join in.

## Ways to help

- **Play and tell us what you think.** What made you smile? What felt confusing?
- **Report a bug.** If something looks wrong or doesn't work, let us know.
- **Share an idea.** A new game, a new level, a cute detail: big or small, we want to hear it.
- **Tell a friend.** Sharing a game you like helps us more than you might think. 🌸

## Reporting a bug 🐞

1. Go to the repository of the game that has the problem (or the [website repository](https://github.com/KumoPlay/kumoplay.github.io) if it's about the website).
2. Open the **Issues** tab and click **New issue**.
3. Choose **Bug report** and fill in what you can. It's okay if you don't know every answer.

Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot. You can drag them right into the form.

> Found a security or privacy problem? Please **don't** open a public issue. Follow our [security policy](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Sharing an idea or feedback 💡

Open a new issue and choose **Game idea or feedback**. Tell us what you'd love to see, or how a game made you feel. We read everything, even if we can't build every idea.

## Contributing code, art, or text 🎨

We're happy to get help with code, art, sound, and writing.

1. **Start with an issue** for anything bigger than a small fix, so we can chat about it before you spend time on it.
2. **Keep changes small and focused.** One fix or feature per pull request is easiest to review.
3. **Keep it cozy.** Our games are for players of all ages, from young kids to adults. Please keep everything gentle, kind, and family-friendly.
4. **Check it on a phone.** Most players are on their phones, often with one hand. Please make sure your change works on a small screen.
5. **Think about everyone.** Add alt text to images, make sure things work with a keyboard, and don't rely on color alone to show meaning.
6. **Open a pull request** and fill in the short template. We'll take a look as soon as we can.

By contributing, you agree that your work is shared under the license of the repository you're contributing to.

## Adding a game to the website

Each game has its own page on [kumoplay.github.io](https://kumoplay.github.io). To add one, follow the step-by-step guide in the website repository: [ADDING_A_GAME.md](https://github.com/KumoPlay/kumoplay.github.io/blob/main/ADDING_A_GAME.md).

## Being kind

Everyone taking part in KumoPlay agrees to follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). In short: be kind, be patient, and help everyone feel welcome.

Thank you for floating by! ☁️
